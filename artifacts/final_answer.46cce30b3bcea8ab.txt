### Root Cause Analysis

| Hypothesis | Symptoms Explained | Evidence to Confirm | Evidence to Reject |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Model/Kernel Bug** | Stalled GPU (low util), 60s timeouts, restart helps | Profiler shows stalled CUDA kernels or infinite loops | GPU utilization remains low with no stalled kernels ||
| **Memory Leak** | Increasing latency, stalled workers, restart helps | Growing GPU/host memory over time | Constant memory footprint ||
| **Batching/Scheduling** | Latency/util mismatch | Incorrect batch shapes/quantization settings | Correct shapes/quantization ||

### Investigation Order

1. **GPU Kernel Profiling**: Highest probability. Check for stalled kernels, deadlocks, or NaN propagation.
2. **Memory Tracking**: Monitor GPU/host memory growth
