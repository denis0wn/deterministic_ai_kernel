## Sourcing filters (how to find rows)

1. **Job boards:** postings for "platform engineer LLM", "AI tooling",
   "dev productivity AI" at fintech/health/insurance companies — a team
   hiring for that is running LLM automation and will need to answer for it.
2. **Changelog readers:** companies shipping "AI-assisted migration" or
   "AI code review" features — they need proof for their own customers.
3. **EU AI Act exposure:** any company whose docs mention CE marking,
   conformity assessment, or "responsible AI" pages — Art. 12 record-keeping
   lands Dec 2027; our evidence pack IS the record.
4. **Agencies/consultancies** advertising "AI-accelerated delivery" — they
   need proof-of-work artifacts for client sign-off.

## The note (template, < 90 words)

> Subject: can your AI fixes prove themselves?
>
> We measured [their task class if known, else "a money-rounding fix"]:
> frontier models fail it 0/14 unassisted. Our kernel completes it 14/14 —
> with an evidence chain per run that your reviewers can verify without
> trusting us. Every claim in this email links a committed, re-runnable
> artifact: [repo link]. Free week-0 evaluation on a sample of your task
> class — you keep the evidence either way. Worth 20 minutes?

## Rules

- One task class per row. "They're a big company" is not a pain.
- Never paste their code anywhere — the week-0 proof runs on THEIR sample
  in THEIR environment or on our fixture.
- Log every reply in this table (date, outcome, next step).
