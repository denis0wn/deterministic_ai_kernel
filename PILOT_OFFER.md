# Pilot Offer — Auditable AI Code-Fixing, with Proof

**One page. What you get, what it costs, how we prove it.**

## For whom

Engineering and platform teams that want LLM-driven code fixes in
production but cannot answer their own reviewers: *"show me exactly what
the model was told, what it answered, what ran, and why this result is
trustworthy."* Especially teams with compliance, audit, or EU AI Act
record-keeping exposure (Art. 12 logging obligations are in force).

## What you get in 4 weeks

1. **Your task class, kernelized.** We pick one recurring, painful code-fix
   or migration task class from your codebase (examples: API migrations,
   rounding/monetary fixes, dependency upgrades, schema changes) and run it
   through the deterministic kernel.

2. **Evidence pack per run.** Every task execution produces a portable
   evidence bundle: the plan, every model prompt and response (with seed),
   the applied patch, the real test-suite report, and a verifiable
   evidence chain. Your reviewers check our claims without trusting us.

3. **Honesty guarantee, demonstrated.** The system never reports success
   it cannot prove. We will show you live failure episodes — the kernel
   refusing to certify a wrong fix — because that behavior is the product.

4. **Deployment your way.** Runs fully local (Apple Silicon, MLX-served
   open models — nothing leaves your machine) or against your cloud
   endpoint. Your code and prompts never pass through us.

## Proof before you pay anything

Week 0: we run our reference defect class on your call — unassisted
frontier model vs. kernel+Layer-1 hints, side by side, measured on real
test suites. You keep the evidence packs either way.
